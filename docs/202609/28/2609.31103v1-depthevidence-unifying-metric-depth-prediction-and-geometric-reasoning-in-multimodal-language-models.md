## 摘要
带有度量约束的空间推理需要将物体与几何测量关联起来，并在语言推理过程中保留其数值内容。我们提出了 DepthEvidence，一个 4B 模型，它使用自身的稠密度量预测作为对象接地的证据来生成语言。一个以相机为条件的解码器利用多尺度视觉特征和高分辨率 RGB 细化来预测全分辨率度量深度。一个稠密到语言接口将预测深度和解码器特征转换为锚定到对象标识符的、对象对齐的连续几何 token。几何监督促使度量信息在语言上下文交互前后保持可恢复，而指令微调支持物体测量和组合推理。我们提出了 Depth-VQA 基准，用于评估物体深度查询、相对比较以及结合空间和数值约束的决策。在九个数据集上，DepthEvidence 在被评估方法中取得了最高的平均稠密 δ1，与专用估计器相比具有竞争力。它还在实例级度量深度估计以及相对和度量推理赛道上的总体准确率方面领先于被评估方法，同时广泛保持通用 VQA 性能，并相对基础模型提升了空间理解。

## Abstract
Spatial reasoning with metric constraints requires linking objects to geometric measurements and preserving their numerical content during language reasoning. We present DepthEvidence, a 4B model that uses its own dense metric predictions as object-grounded evidence for language generation. A camera-conditioned decoder predicts full-resolution metric depth using multi-scale visual features and high-resolution RGB refinement. A dense-to-language interface converts predicted depths and decoder features into object-aligned continuous geometry tokens anchored to object identifiers. Geometric supervision encourages metric information to remain recoverable before and after language-context interaction, while instruction tuning supports object measurement and compositional reasoning. We introduce a Depth-VQA benchmark evaluating object-depth queries, relative comparisons, and decisions combining spatial and numerical constraints. Across nine datasets, DepthEvidence achieves the highest average dense $δ_1$ among evaluated methods, competitive with specialized estimators. It also leads the evaluated methods in instance-level metric depth estimation and overall accuracy on both relative and metric reasoning tracks, while broadly preserving general VQA performance and improving spatial understanding relative to the base model.
